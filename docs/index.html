Models. H3 = Q_AV (Qwen2.5-Omni-7B + LoRA) + A3 (Chinese HuBERT) + V2 (CLIP ViT-L/14), frozen fusion and calibration.

CH-SIMS v2. Sentiment labels, not seven emotions. Test n = 1,034; 976 with media. Pearson r 0.792, cluster-bootstrap 95% CI [0.756, 0.829]. All-clip r 0.761. At the frozen threshold 645 of 1,034 clips are accepted (62.4%); 257 of the rejections are multi-face film scenes.

EmotionTalk. 1,929 test utterances from four speakers kept out of training. Macro-F1 0.645 (no rejection); 0.642 at 88.5% coverage with the frozen threshold, abstentions counted as errors; 0.624 with automatic segmentation at 87.66% coverage. Bubbles show F1 with automatic segmentation.

Latency. 2.21 s is the median from the detected end of speech to output (P95 2.80 s). From the true end of an utterance to its last output: 3.11 s median, 11.02 s P95, because some utterances are merged into one segment. Wall-clock to media ratio 1.001 over a three-hour replay. No claim for mobile, robot hardware or long-duration use.
