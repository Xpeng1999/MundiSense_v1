01 / Approach

More than one signal.
More than a label.

EmotionCore combines an audio-visual model with speech and facial-expression specialists. Frozen fusion and decision rules turn their outputs into a consistent perception interface.

01

Hear and see together.

The default H3 path combines Qwen2.5-Omni-based audio-visual predictions with audio and visual specialists.

Speech + facial expression

02

Leave room for uncertainty.

Return an emotion label and confidence, fall back to audio when no face is available, or abstain under the configured rejection rules.

Confidence + fallback + rejection

03

Follow a conversation over time.

A streaming prototype segments speech and emits timestamped events. Recorded-stream replay provides an initial view of latency and stability.

Segmented speech → timestamped events

Measurement notes and evidence provenance

Frozen system. H3 uses Q_AV, A3 and V2 component probabilities with the v1.1 calibration bundle and X06b stream configuration. The overview above is a schematic of this system, not a live prediction or the future intermediate-fusion architecture described in Mundiscope's business plan.

CH-SIMS v2. Test n = 1,034; H3 media-available n = 976. Pearson r = 0.792257; video-cluster bootstrap 95% CI [0.756380, 0.828748], 2,000 replicates. All-clip Pearson r = 0.761319. Binary sentiment accuracy = 81.888% on 911 nonzero-label clips, with unavailable media counted as errors. CH-SIMS has sentiment labels, not the seven emotion categories. X07 results are documented on the lock-box branch; inclusion here does not merge that experiment branch.

EmotionTalk. X06b R3 evaluates 1,929 complete utterances: H3 OP-N macro-F1 = 0.645131. At OP-R90, macro-F1 = 0.641794 and coverage = 88.491%. The sentence-level comparison does not apply the multi-face gate; rejected predictions count in the F1 denominator. The R4 automatic-VAD evaluation on dialogue-duration streams yields macro-F1 = 0.624027 and coverage = 87.662%. These results do not establish multi-person performance.

Streaming. X06b R5 uses three hours of recorded-stream replay on an RTX 5090 32 GB workstation, not a live camera benchmark. Annotated utterance-end to last-overlapping-event output P50/P95 = 3.107789/11.016996 s, over 1,929 utterances. This includes waiting for merged or split speech segments; it is not time to first feedback or a natural-user latency benchmark. The shorter planned VAD endpoint-to-output P50/P95 = 2.213491/2.804833 s; wall-clock/media ratio = 1.001054; backlog P95 = 0.998267 s. Process memory grows about 11% before stabilizing, and longer-duration deployment remains to be evaluated.

Abstention. OP-R90 is the name of a development-calibrated operating point. It does not guarantee 90% coverage after a domain shift. On CH-SIMS v2 test, coverage is 645/1,034 = 62.379%, with accepted-clip Pearson r = 0.810012.

What this preview does and does not include

EmotionCore is a research prototype for observable emotional expression. Facial and vocal cues do not reveal a person's private thoughts or establish a mental-health diagnosis. Current evaluation is limited to recorded datasets and stream replay.

The preview does not include a public upload service, model-weight download, commercial license, robot hardware, ROS 2/gRPC integration, persistent interaction memory, or an autonomous robot behavior system. Those items are not demonstrated by the present results. Pose and touch sensing belong to the broader Mundiscope direction rather than this prototype.
